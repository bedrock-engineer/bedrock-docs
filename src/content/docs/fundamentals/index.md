**Where this fits:** [Docs Atlas](../index.md) › Fundamentals

A [fundamental](../reference/glossary.md#fundamental) explains a concept that recurs across structures, analyses and data, rather than belonging to one of them. Two groups are planned.

**Geomechanics:** effective stress, drained versus undrained behavior, consolidation and shear strength. Until they get pages of their own, the glossary covers them: [effective stress](../reference/glossary.md#effective-stress), [friction angle](../reference/glossary.md#friction-angle) and [undrained shear strength](../reference/glossary.md#undrained-shear-strength).

**Geospatial data:** spatial reference systems, turning ground investigation data into geospatial data, and the hierarchical structure of that data. These pages are in the bedrock.engineer docs now and will move here.

An explanation that belongs to one analysis method goes on that method's page in [Geotechnical analyses](../geotechnical-analyses/index.md), as [Bearing capacity](../geotechnical-analyses/bearing-capacity.mdx) does.
