[Ground investigation](../reference/glossary.md#gi) finds out what the ground and groundwater are like at and around a site. What to investigate, and in how much detail, follows from planning: it is whatever the ground model still lacks for the analysis methods the checks need.

In the first project phase the ground investigation available is existing data. In the Netherlands that means the CPTs and boreholes in the BRO, national models such as [GeoTOP](../reference/glossary.md#geotop), and earlier investigations on or near the site. Then comes new fieldwork: boreholes with samples and lab tests, [CPTs](../reference/glossary.md#cpt) and geophysics. During construction, monitoring adds new data.

Planned pages: existing data from the BRO and GeoTOP, boreholes and sampling, lab tests, CPTs, geophysics and monitoring.

To look at CPTs and borehole logs in a notebook, see [cpt-anywidget](../tools/cpt-anywidget/index.md).
