Ground modeling turns the [ground investigation](../reference/glossary.md#gi) data into an [engineering geological model](../reference/glossary.md#egm): which soil and rock units are where, how they formed, how they behave, and where the groundwater is. The EGM describes the ground only. The structure comes in with the geotechnical design model, in [Geotechnical analyses](../geotechnical-analyses/index.md).

How detailed the model must be, and how far it must reach, follows from the analysis methods it serves.

Planned pages: stratigraphy from CPTs and boreholes, using national models such as [GeoTOP](../reference/glossary.md#geotop), and geostatistics with [variograms](../reference/glossary.md#variogram) and [conditional simulation](../reference/glossary.md#conditional-simulation) to show how uncertain the ground is between data points.

The worked example [Soft soil EGM](../worked-examples/soft-soil-egm/index.md) builds a probabilistic EGM from GeoTOP and CPTs.
