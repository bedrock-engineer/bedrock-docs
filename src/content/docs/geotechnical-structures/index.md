Planning starts here. A [geotechnical structure page](../reference/glossary.md#structure-page) lists the [checks](../reference/glossary.md#check) for one structure: its own [limit states](../reference/glossary.md#limit-state), and its effects on its surroundings, during construction and in service. Each check says when it applies and which [geotechnical analysis](../geotechnical-analyses/index.md) answers it.

The structures follow the clauses of EN 1997-3:2025, plus four that other codes cover. The numbers count the checks on the structure itself per analysis type. Checks on the surroundings are still to be added.

| Structure | Stability | Deformation | Groundwater flow | Dynamics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slopes, cuttings and embankments | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| [Spread foundations](spread-foundations.md) | 4 | 2 | 1 | 1 |

| Anchors | 3 | 1 |  |  |
| Reinforced fill structures | 3 | 2 |  |  |
| Soil nailed structures | 2 | 1 | 1 |  |
| Rock bolts and rock surface support | 2 | 1 |  | 1 |
| Ground improvement | 1 | 3 |  | 1 |
| Groundwater control |  | 1 | 5 |  |
| Underground structures | 2 | 3 | 1 | 1 |
| Dams | 2 | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| Pavements and subgrades | 1 | 3 | 1 | 1 |

So far only [Spread foundations](spread-foundations.md) has a page.
